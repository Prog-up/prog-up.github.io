### Mission Overview
During the summer of 2023, I worked as a seasonal logistics associate at **Agrial** in Douvres-la-Délivrande. Agrial is a major French agricultural cooperative, and my role was situated at the core of their retail and supply chain operations.

### Key Responsibilities
- **Inventory Management:** Conducted daily audits of stock levels, ensuring accurate tracking of incoming agricultural supplies and consumer goods.
- **Order Fulfillment:** Handled the picking, packing, and staging of customer orders with strict adherence to safety and quality protocols.
- **Merchandising:** Maintained shop floor layouts, organized displays, and ensured that products were accessible and properly rotated (FIFO).

### Takeaways
While outside my core focus of SecDevOps and AI architecture, this experience reinforced critical soft skills such as operating efficiently under pressure, maintaining rigorous organizational standards, and adapting quickly to fast-paced operational environments.
